# Kamino Lending operations in Lifeboat

On the Kamino screen, tap “Refresh”. The app checks that the RPC endpoint is on Solana mainnet-beta and shows any obligations it finds, along with debt, collateral, reserves, and reasons an action may be unavailable. For a supported position, choose debt repayment or collateral withdrawal, enter an amount in token units or `all`, review the simulation, and confirm the signature.

For repayment from a token ATA, `balance` (the **Wallet balance** command in the dialog) offers the whole ATA balance read during preparation. Kamino settles the minimum of the debt and the offered amount, so a balance larger than the debt repays the full debt and keeps the rest in the wallet; a smaller balance makes a partial repayment. The simulated debit is shown for review. An empty ATA fails before simulation. `balance` is not available for native SOL repayment, which would otherwise spend the SOL needed for rent and fees. `all` (**Full debt**) still requests the protocol's full-debt sentinel and requires zero residual debt in simulation.

WSOL debt can be repaid from a WSOL associated token account (ATA) or from native SOL. In the latter case, a temporary WSOL account is created and closed in the same transaction. Withdrawn WSOL remains WSOL. Refresh the position after repayment before withdrawing collateral.

For a position with debt, the details pane shows Kamino's own risk values: LTV (borrow-factor-adjusted debt over collateral value), max LTV, liquidation LTV and health (liquidation threshold value over adjusted debt; Kamino can liquidate at or below 1.00). They are ratios of the values KLend stored at the position's last on-chain refresh, so the note gives their approximate age; prices and interest may have moved since. The app does not revalue the position. The withdrawal review shows the same values recomputed from the refresh simulation that precedes it, with current interest and Scope prices, as they stand before the withdrawal. Lifeboat never blocks an operation based on these values. Kamino's own max-LTV check in the simulation decides whether a withdrawal can proceed.

Positions in an elevation group (eMode) use the same commands. Kamino then applies the group's LTV to every collateral, so the `all` withdrawal is sized with the group LTV, which is often higher than the reserve's own. If the group has new loans disabled, Kamino blocks collateral withdrawal until the debt is repaid; the app reports this before simulation. Eleven LST reserves (for example hubSOL and bnSOL) count as collateral only inside SOL eMode (group 2) and are offered for withdrawal only there.

Kamino Multiply positions (obligation tag 1) use the same commands; KLend treats them like any other obligation when repaying or withdrawing. Lifeboat unwinds them only with funds already in the wallet: repay the debt (for example WSOL debt with native SOL), refresh, then withdraw the collateral. It does not swap collateral or use flash loans, so a position whose debt exceeds the wallet's funds can only be repaid in part, and with debt remaining the withdrawable collateral is limited by the LTV. The details pane notes when a position is Multiply or in eMode.

Support is limited to the pinned main market, vanilla (tag 0) and Multiply (tag 1) obligations inside or outside an elevation group, and classic SPL Token reserves: 29 for repayment and 40 for withdrawal. Positions with active obligation orders (price- or LTV-triggered deleveraging) remain unsupported. Available operations and exclusions are listed in the [reserve matrix](kamino-reserve-matrix.md); KLend, Scope, and Farms versions are recorded in [interface provenance](kamino-interface-provenance.md). An action appearing on screen does not guarantee execution: the app checks accounts again and simulates the full transaction before signing.

The [shared SPL/Token-2022 layer](token-infrastructure.md) is currently used by Meteora. It does not enable Token-2022 reserves or new obligation types in Kamino. Future Kamino integration needs separate verification of reserve CPI accounts, extension permissions, net repayment/debt cancellation, repay-all fee gross-up, collateral redemption and withheld vault liquidity.

The RPC endpoint must support filtered `getProgramAccounts` with context, `getMultipleAccounts` with `minContextSlot`, and simulation that returns account data. If the submission outcome is uncertain, a new operation is blocked until the saved signature is checked. The migration history from `Lifeboat-cli` is in the [Kamino plan](kamino-mobile-plan.md).
